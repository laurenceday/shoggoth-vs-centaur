# Responsibility matrix

This matrix compares repository-owned responsibilities at the two registered
commits in the [pin registry](../evidence/pins.json). Status terms retain the
[methodology's meanings](00-methodology.md#claim-statuses), and the complete
evidence inventory is in the [source ledger](SOURCES.md). Each row names a
responsibility difference; it does not turn unlike layers into one product
category.

## Evidence and reading limits

- [Current] The two source editions and read modes are fixed in the
  [source ledger](SOURCES.md#edition-pins-and-observation-boundary); a later
  default branch does not silently update these rows.
- [Reported] Open records remain reports or proposals with their observation
  dates and non-reproduction boundaries in the [source ledger](SOURCES.md).
- [Unknown] Neither system was deployed for this study. Runtime correctness,
  scale, latency, cost, model quality, adoption, and private overlays or hosts
  remain outside what the [registered evidence](SOURCES.md#unknowns) proves.
