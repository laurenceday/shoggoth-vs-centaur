# ADR-001: Compare Shoggoth and Centaur at the layers they occupy

## Status

Accepted, 2026-08-26. A future decision record must mark this one superseded
when it stops being true.

## Context

Shoggoth is distributed as governed specialist skills and a receipted
repository-delivery loop. Centaur is distributed as a self-hosted team-agent
service with durable state and execution infrastructure. A flat feature list
would treat a capability outside one project's claimed layer as a defect and
would turn unlike responsibilities into an unexplained ranking.

This comparison must preserve exact source pins, visible claim statuses, a
no-integration boundary, a private proprietary rights position, and the human
contributor's authorship and signing identity.

## Decision

Compare the projects by named responsibility at their actual layers, retain
their genuine overlap, and describe complement and competition separately
without scores or a winner.

## Alternatives

### One long README

It would minimise file count, but mix method, subject profiles, synthesis, and
evidence into one review surface. Qualifications would drift away from the
claims they bound.

### JSON-first catalogue with generated Markdown

It could enforce exhaustive fields, but would make a generator and schema a
second product. The comparison's qualifications do not reduce cleanly to
fields.

### Visual scorecard

It would scan quickly, but hide purpose inside unexplained weights and imply a
universal ranking the evidence cannot support.

## Consequences

The repository uses modular documents, symmetric questions, exact source
pins, and explicit status labels. Readers can challenge the method, one
subject, the synthesis, or the evidence independently.

This makes maintenance more deliberate: a pin change requires coordinated
review of the registry, source links, and affected prose. It also forbids the
shorter but misleading outputs of an aggregate score, winner statement, or
combined architecture. Reversing the method requires a superseding ADR.
